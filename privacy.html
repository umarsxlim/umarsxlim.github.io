Privacy

FeedClear is a Shopify app operated by TheGem (thegemfoundry@gmail.com).

We store the shop domain, product fields required to scan Google Shopping rules, optional Google Merchant Center tokens if you connect Google, and billing status. We do not sell merchant data. Uninstall deletes stored shop data.

Google OAuth, if connected, is used only to read Merchant Center diagnostics and is not cached from the Issue Resolution API.
