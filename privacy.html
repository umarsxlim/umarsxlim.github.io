Privacy

Last updated 26 September 2026.

FeedClear is a Shopify app operated by TheGem. Canonical policy: feedclear.vercel.app/privacy.

We collect shop domain, Shopify app session information, installed app scopes, catalog fields read through the GraphQL Admin API for pre-flight checks, and billing status from the Shopify Billing API. We never collect card numbers.

FeedClear pre-flight findings are generated from observable Shopify catalog data. They are not Google-reported diagnostics. We do not connect Google Merchant Center and we do not collect Merchant Center OAuth tokens.

Supported catalog writes are limited to merchant-confirmed product title and description changes. Uninstall deletes stored Shopify sessions for that shop. We do not sell merchant data.

Contact: support@feedclear.app
